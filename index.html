常见问题 / FAQ

Q: 如何恢复已购买的内容？/ How to restore purchases?

A: 请前往应用内的"设置"页面，点击"恢复购买"按钮即可恢复您之前购买的所有内容。

Go to the "Settings" page in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" to restore all your previous purchases.

Q: 应用闪退怎么办？/ What if the app crashes?

A: 请尝试以下步骤：1. 关闭应用并重新打开；2. 确保您的设备系统是最新版本；3. 删除并重新安装应用。如果问题仍然存在，请联系我们。

Please try: 1. Close and reopen the app; 2. Update your device to the latest OS version; 3. Delete and reinstall the app. If the issue persists, please contact us.

Q: 排行榜分数如何上传？/ How are leaderboard scores uploaded?

A: 在您首次使用排行榜功能时，应用会请求您的同意。只有在您同意后，分数才会上传到我们的服务器。您可以在设置中随时关闭此功能。

The app will ask for your consent when you first use the leaderboard feature. Scores are only uploaded after you agree. You can disable this in Settings at any time.

Q: 如何删除我的数据？/ How to delete my data?

A: 请发送邮件至 your-email@example.com，注明您的请求，我们会在收到后尽快处理。

Please send an email to your-email@example.com with your request, and we will process it as soon as possible.
